# CSRC juniors: personalised circuits

This season, we have created a circuit for you all to do at home. It consists of 10 stations, two of which have been chosen to target something specific that will help you with your rowing technique or performance.

This circuit is optional, we're not going to be checking up on you to see if you have done it. But I'd really encourage you to do this 2/3 times a week throughout the winter, as it will really contribute to your performance during regatta season.

## The circuit

This circuit is a full body circuit, that targets legs, arms, abs and mobility. We'll let you know what your personalised exercises are separately.

I'd suggest the usual - 45 seconds on, 15 seconds off. You can challenge yourself either by finding a more challenging variation of the exercise, or by increasing each station to 1 minute on, 20 second off or similar. Some suggestions for more challenging variations are included below.
